Gita anchor

2.47 — your karma, not the phala

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्तु अकर्मणि॥
You have a right to perform your prescribed duty, but you are not entitled to the fruits of your actions. Never consider yourself the cause of the results of your activities, and never be attached to not doing your duty.

Four parts of the verse

Act — your authority is the action. Showing up. The 5:00 wake. The breath at 10:30. The office presence rebuilt one day at a time.

Release the fruit — outcomes are not yours to grip. Whether the day feels calm, whether the office reset lands, whether BVRC gains traction this quarter.

Drop the doership — don't even take yourself to be the cause of results. This is the antidote to overthinking. Action done with full attention, then released.

Don't withdraw — equanimity is not avoidance. The harder conversations, the visibility you're rebuilding — these are karma you don't get to skip.

For today

One action: do the next thing on the timetable, with full presence, and release it. Not "execute the week perfectly." Just the next thing.

The trip to Digboi already proved this. Karma done, phala took care of itself. Trust the pattern again.
